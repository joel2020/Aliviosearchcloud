Ask any small business owner how many sales they've lost to a competitor, and they'll have a story. Ask them how many they've lost to silence — leads that just never replied, quotes that just never came back — and they'll usually go quiet.

Silence is the number one cause of lost revenue in small business. It's also the most fixable. This is the follow-up system we recommend to every Alivio customer, and what makes the AI version of it actually work.

## Why follow-up is the highest-leverage activity in your business

A few numbers worth pinning to the wall:

- The average B2B sale closes after **5–8 touches**.
- Most salespeople give up after **1 or 2 touches**.
- Increasing follow-up depth from 2 to 5 touches typically lifts close rate by **40–60%**.
- The cost of one more touch is essentially zero. The cost of acquiring a new lead to replace the lost one is hundreds to thousands of dollars.

Follow-up is, by a wide margin, the cheapest pipeline you will ever generate. Every dollar spent on follow-up automation outperforms a dollar spent on ads.

## The anatomy of a real follow-up system

A working follow-up system has five parts. Most SMBs have one or two. The gap is where the revenue is.

### 1. Capture

Every lead — call, form, walk-in, referral, DM — lands in one place with a timestamp, channel, and source. If a lead lives in a notebook on your desk, it's not in the system.

### 2. Tagging

Each lead is tagged by **stage** (new, qualified, quoted, won, lost) and **status** (waiting on customer, waiting on you, no response). Without status, you don't know who to chase.

### 3. Cadence

A predefined sequence of touches. The one we recommend for service businesses:

- **Touch 1 — Same day:** Personalized confirmation + helpful next step.
- **Touch 2 — Day 2:** Useful resource (case study, FAQ, photo of similar work).
- **Touch 3 — Day 4:** Soft check-in. "Anything I can clarify?"
- **Touch 4 — Day 8:** Reframe the value or address a likely objection.
- **Touch 5 — Day 14:** Direct question. "Should I close this out, or do you want to move forward?"
- **Touch 6 — Day 30:** Long-term reactivation. "Circling back — still relevant?"

Cadence is what separates real follow-up from "I emailed them once."

### 4. Channel-matching

Phone leads get a phone follow-up. Form leads get email. WhatsApp leads stay on WhatsApp. Don't make the customer change channels.

### 5. Hand-off

Replies route to a human instantly. Nothing kills follow-up faster than a real reply going to a dead inbox.

## Why humans can't run the system alone

A small team running a service business cannot run a six-touch cadence on every lead. The math doesn't work. With 50 quoted leads a month and 6 touches each, that's 300 messages per month before any actual selling happens.

This is why most SMBs end up with one of two failure modes:

- **The "good lead" bias:** Owner only follows up on the leads that "feel" hot. The rest die. The owner doesn't realize how many were closeable.
- **The "I'll get to it" pile:** Quotes sent, leads tagged, but the actual cadence never executes because there's always something more urgent.

Both modes leave six figures on the table.
